2. The Draconic Moon - The Soul's Emotional Nature:

Interpret the draconic Moon's sign as the person's innate emotional instinct and deepest needs, prior to conditioning. Compare it to the natal Moon's sign and house: does the outward emotional style (natal Moon) support or mask the soul-level emotional nature (draconic Moon)?

For each, note the sign and any draconic-to-draconic aspects, then bring in the corresponding draconic-to-natal aspects to show how (or whether) this soul-level quality is finding expression through the outward personality.

4. Draconic Outer Planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto):

These move slowly, so draconic and natal placements are often close in sign. Only give them significant space when the sign differs from natal, or when a draconic-to-natal aspect involves a personal planet or angle (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Ascendant, Midheaven) - those are the moments where a generational, soul-deep theme is being personally and specifically activated.

- Squares and oppositions: friction between the soul's native disposition and the personality that circumstance built - a place where the outward self resists, contradicts, or has yet to catch up with the deeper self. Frame this as fertile tension and growth-edge, not pathology.

Always name which draconic planet and which natal planet are involved, and describe the lived, felt texture of that specific inner-outer dynamic.
